The `capacity` of `HeldCapacity` accepts a piecewise-constant calendar schedule such as `Steps`, as the `budget_per_period` of `CapacityConstrained` does. Places can open or close over an outbreak. Each stay is checked against the capacity from its start to its end. When the capacity falls below the places held, no stay is ended and new admissions are refused until occupancy is below the new level. `DeferRefused` waits for the first moment a release or a rise in capacity leaves a place free.
