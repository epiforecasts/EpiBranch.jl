A `MassVaccination` under `CapacityConstrained` gives its doses to the population, and the people they protect leave the susceptibles, in `simulate_by_generation` and on a `BranchingProcess` with a `Population`. A `PerGroup` budget doses one type. The new `dosing` field, an `AbstractDosing` that defaults to `RandomDosing()`, decides how a batch of doses is spread; a new rule defines `EpiBranch.protected_by_doses`.
