`MixingMeanFieldProcess` and `MixingLinearNoiseProcess` now read a vaccination campaign from the interventions of the `ModelSpec` instead of refusing it: a `GroupVaccination`, alone or under `Scheduled` (a `start_time` and/or `end_time`) and `CapacityConstrained` (a fixed budget or a schedule such as `Steps`), becomes doses into each mixing group's compartments. `group_key` (which must be one of the `mixing_by` keys), `efficacy` and `mode`, `coverage`, `dose_delay` and the budget are read from the intervention, so nothing is specified twice. Doses reach people whatever their status, and only those that fall on unvaccinated susceptibles protect anyone. An all-or-nothing dose makes a share `efficacy` of them immune; a leaky dose moves them to a vaccinated-susceptible compartment with the hazard scaled by `1 - efficacy`. A mode with no such model, `waning`, `delay_to_immunity`, a `DeferRefused` budget, a `Scheduled` gated on cases and any other intervention are refused.

A new `DoseAllocation` sets how a group's discrete trigger and a shared budget become doses. The default, `TriggeredInPriority`, follows the exact model: a group is triggered once one eligible case is expected and offered doses `dose_delay` later. Triggered groups draw on the budget in the order they were triggered. `SplitByGroupSize` and `SplitBySusceptibles` share the budget's daily rate over the groups, ignoring the trigger. `HybridProcess` passes the trigger times, the doses given and who they protected from its exact phase to the default at the switch.

Both approximations take `immune = ImmuneShare(...)` or `ImmuneCount(...)` in `simulate`, a per-group share or number of people immune at `t = 0`, taken out of the susceptibles before the initial infections. The states gain `V` (dosed susceptibles), `P` (immune) and `D` (doses given) and the progress of the campaign, and the linear noise covariance covers `(S, I, V, P)`, with `S` and `I` first as before.
