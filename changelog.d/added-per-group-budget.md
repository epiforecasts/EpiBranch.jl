`PerGroup` gives a `CapacityConstrained` a budget for each group, as a number or a schedule: `budget_per_period = PerGroup(:under5 => 10.0, :over5 => Steps(...))`. The budget is part of the intervention, and the individual-based simulation, `simulate_by_generation`, `MixingMeanFieldProcess`, `MixingLinearNoiseProcess` and `HybridProcess` all read it. The groups are the values of the `group_key` of a `GroupVaccination`, or of the `key` that the `PerGroup` names for an intervention without one. A group missing from the mapping raises an `ArgumentError`. An extension author can give an intervention a group with `EpiBranch.budget_group_key`. A new page, "What each engine supports", tabulates which engine applies which intervention, and `test/test_engine_support.jl` checks each engine against it.
