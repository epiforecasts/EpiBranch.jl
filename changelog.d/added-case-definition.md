`ModelSpec` takes a `case_definition`: a rule, either a function `history -> label` or a subtype of `AbstractCaseDefinition` with an `EpiBranch.classify_case` method, that classifies each case from the events recorded on them. It is evaluated at every time an event was recorded, with the events up to that time visible through `event_time` and `has_happened`, so the classification changes over time. Events recorded late, such as tracing, reach it. `case_classification_changes(state)` lists each change with its time and `linelist` shows the final classification as `case_classification`. `ClinicalCaseDefinition` (confirmed, probable or suspected from a test result, symptoms and a known contact) and `FixedProportionConfirmed` are provided. `ClinicalCaseDefinition` reads the `:test_time` and `:test_result` that `Testing` records, so a model with no `Testing` step has no confirmed cases under it.
