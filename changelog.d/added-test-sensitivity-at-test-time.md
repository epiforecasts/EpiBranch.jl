`Isolation`'s `test_sensitivity` accepts a function `(rng, ind, t) -> probability` and is drawn once the test time `t` is known. Test positivity can then follow the within-host load of the case at that time. The asymptomatic cases tutorial now shows onset as a hazard on the load, and how to calibrate a trajectory model to an incubation period and a serial interval, and names the parameters that those two distributions leave unidentified.
