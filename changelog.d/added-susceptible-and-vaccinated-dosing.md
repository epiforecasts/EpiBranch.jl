Two new `AbstractDosing` rules. `SusceptibleDosing` gives every dose to a susceptible. `FavourVaccinated(weight)` makes a person already protected `weight` times as likely as anyone else to receive each dose. `EpiBranch.protected_by_doses` takes the number of people already protected as a new argument before `size`. `GenerationCounts` gains a `protected` field that counts them.
