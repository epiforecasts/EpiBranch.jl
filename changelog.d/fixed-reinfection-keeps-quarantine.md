A reinfection no longer clears a quarantine already standing on the person. The engine now calls a new per-episode hook, `initialise_episode!` (default: nothing), instead of `initialise_individual!` on a reinfected case. `Isolation` uses it to redraw `:test_positive` and end its own earlier self-isolation, while `ContactTracing`'s `:quarantined` state and any quarantine stretch stay in place.
