The count-level path of `simulate` now runs a model with a finite population of one group. It thins each day's contacts against the susceptibles left, as `PerContactDepletion` does. It also runs a `ModelSpec` with `GroupVaccination` under `CapacityConstrained` (and `Scheduled` with a window), giving each day's doses to the susceptibles. `CountSimulationState` holds the susceptibles left. The doses approximate the individual simulation, as in `simulate_by_generation`.
