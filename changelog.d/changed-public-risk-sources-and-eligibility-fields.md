`EpiBranch.HostImmunity` and `EpiBranch.AbortedInfection` are now `public`, and `EpiBranch.infinite_draw_is_final` is a documented kernel trait. A kernel written outside the package that returns `Inf` once its total hazard is exhausted declares the trait to skip the bisection retry. `Isolation` now takes its required fields from `required_fields(eligibility)`, with `required_fields(::SymptomaticOnly) = [:asymptomatic]`. A custom `IsolationEligibility` therefore extends the public `required_fields`. The internal `_required_for_eligibility` is removed.
