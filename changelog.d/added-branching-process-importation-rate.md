The `importations` keyword of `BranchingProcess` also takes a rate of imports per day: a number, a calendar schedule (`Steps`, `Seasonal`, or any type with a `calendar_multiplier` method), or a function of the day returning the rate. Arrivals are drawn as a Poisson process over `max_time`, which a rate requires. Each arrival is an unseeded index case with `:infection_route = :external`. Capacity limits, `max_cases`, `max_time` and depletion of susceptibles apply as they do for a vector of times.
