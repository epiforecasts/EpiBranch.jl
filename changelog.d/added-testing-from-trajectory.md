`Testing` takes a test from a case's own within-host trajectory (`within_host`), mapping its value at the sampling time to a reading and a result through a user-supplied `Assay`. The trajectory can be any quantity measured over time since infection, such as load, titre or culture-measured infectiousness. Each test has a `name` (`:test` by default) that sets its keys and line-list columns, so a case can have several tests. `Isolation`'s `test_sensitivity` can be a `Testing` step; numbers, distributions and functions work as before.
