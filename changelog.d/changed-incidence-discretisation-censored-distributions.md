`incidence_moments` now discretises the generation time and the reporting delay with CensoredDistributions.jl's `double_interval_censored` (a primary event uniform over the step, whole-step secondary interval) instead of integrating with `quadgk`. CensoredDistributions.jl is now a direct dependency rather than a weak one, and the serial-interval extension loads on ConvolvedDistributions.jl alone. A fixed delay (a number, or a `Dirac`) is binned in closed form. `Dual` generation-time and delay parameters still differentiate.
