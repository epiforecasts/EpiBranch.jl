`Isolation` treated a traced contact's standing quarantine as a competing
pathway and only acted when its own onset-based isolation would have started
earlier, so a contact who developed symptoms after a quarantine had started,
or after a short quarantine had already lapsed, was never isolated at onset.
It now always layers isolation from onset over the quarantine: merged into
one removal where the two overlap, and recorded as a separate removal where
they do not.
