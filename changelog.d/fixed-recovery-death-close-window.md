`Recovery` and `Death` now close an infectious window the way a terminal `Transition` does. The winning outcome is recorded under its own state and `<state>_time` key (`:recovered_time`, `:died_time`), so a window listing `:recovered` or `:died` in `until` closes there. Before, an SIR model with `Recovery` ran as SI, and contacts after a `Death` were not censored. A candidate that loses to an earlier outcome closes nothing.
