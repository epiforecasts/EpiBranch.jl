`incidence_moments` now keeps the generation-interval mass that falls in the same step as the infector, as infections caused within the step, instead of dropping it and renormalising. This removes the bias in the moments when the generation time is short relative to the step. The offspring of the seed cases follow the same rule: those infected in step 0 are not observed but go on to infect later steps. A generation time so short, or an offspring mean so large, that the expected number of infections within a step is unbounded now throws an `ArgumentError` asking for a shorter step.
