`simulate_by_generation` and the mixing approximations now read what an intervention does to a group through one dispatched trait, `EpiBranch.count_dosing`, and one reader of `Scheduled` and `CapacityConstrained` wrappers, instead of each testing for `GroupVaccination` itself. An intervention written outside the package opts in by defining `count_dosing`; every other intervention is refused in the same words by every model. A new page, "What each model applies", tabulates what each kind of model applies, and `test/test_engine_support.jl` checks every kind against the table.
