A new trait, `prevents_contact`, lets an intervention say whether an infection it blocks was ever a contact. A measure that stops the infection while people still meet, such as a mask or a partly effective vaccine, keeps the blocked contact in `contacts()` and within reach of tracing, as before. One that stops the meeting itself, such as isolation in hospital or a closed school, now drops it. The branching process and models written against `contacts_of` remove it from the infector's contacts, tracing does not reach it, and a recorder on a fixed-population model is not offered it. The default is `false`. `Isolation` and `Quarantine` default to `true` and take `prevents_contact` as a keyword, so `Isolation(...; prevents_contact = false)` is isolation at home, where contacts continue and the blocked ones are still recorded.
