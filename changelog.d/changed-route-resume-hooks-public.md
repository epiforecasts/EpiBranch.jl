`EpiBranch.resume_route!`, `EpiBranch.resumable_route`, `EpiBranch.MassAction` and `EpiBranch.mass_action_schedule` are now public, replacing the underscored `_resume_route!`, `_resumable_route`, `_MassAction` and `_mass_action_schedule`. A contact structure written outside the package can now reuse the mass-action route and redraw a capped window on resume, as `HomogeneousProcess` and `MixingProcess` do.
