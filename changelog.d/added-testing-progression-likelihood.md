`progression_loglik` now scores a `Testing` step: the probability and timing of the sample, as for `Reporting` (including censoring and `truncated`), and for a test that was taken, the probability of the recorded result and the density of the recorded reading given the trajectory of the case at the sampling time. `measure_loglik(assay, value, reading, positive)` is the reverse of `measure`. A numeric `detect` on `Assay` is an exact limit, `DetectionProbability(p)` gives a probabilistic result that can be scored, and a plain `detect` function throws because it has no probability to evaluate. A new `noise` distribution on `Assay` (default `nothing`, so simulations are unchanged) is added to the reading and gives it a density.
