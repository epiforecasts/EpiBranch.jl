`multiplier` is now a keyword of `BranchingProcess` and `immune` a keyword of `Population`. `simulate_by_generation` and `simulate(model, state::GenerationCounts)` no longer take them. An individual run of a model with a `multiplier` draws it once per generation and scales the offspring mean of every case in that generation. The counts of a generation follow the same law as in `simulate_by_generation`. The people in the `immune` of a population are never infected in an individual run. A model with either keyword no longer takes the daily count-level path. A model other than a `BranchingProcess` raises an error for a nonzero `immune`.
