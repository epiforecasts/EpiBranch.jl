`simulate` also runs a branching process with a `PoissonLognormal` or `PoissonWeibull` offspring law as daily counts. The total infectiousness of a cohort is the sum of one draw per member. The state keeps these draws to share out the infections of the cohort when its people are built.
`CountRule` now takes the distribution `ν` of the infectiousness of one member instead of a gamma shape `k`. `CountRule(R, k, g)` with a number `k` still works. For these two laws the time and memory of the count-level path grow with the number of cases, because the draws of the members are kept.
