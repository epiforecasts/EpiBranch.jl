`RenewalOffspring(R, generation_time; k)` is an offspring function for a time-varying instantaneous reproduction number `R(t)`, as in the renewal equation. A case draws an individual infectiousness from a `Gamma(k, 1/k)` and infects people at a rate proportional to `R(t)` times the generation-time density, so that with a constant `R` the offspring distribution is `NegBin(R, k)`. The generation time is a continuous distribution (infection times are continuous, drawn by thinning against `R_max`) or a vector of weights over daily lags. It returns an `OffspringTimes`, which `Infectiousness`'s `calendar` cannot do, since that scales the contact kernel's hazard and so changes when contacts happen, not how many there are.
