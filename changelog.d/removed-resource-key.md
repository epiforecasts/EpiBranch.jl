`EpiBranch.resource_key` is removed. A budget is split by group through the groups of the `Population`, as `PerGroup` as `budget_per_period` of `CapacityConstrained` or as `capacity` of `HeldCapacity`, so an intervention written outside the package needs no method to have a budget for each district or centre. `capacity_usage` and `held_usage` take the name of the group as their `key`.
