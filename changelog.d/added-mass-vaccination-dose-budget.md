A `MassVaccination` under `CapacityConstrained` gives its doses to the population in `simulate_by_generation` and on a `BranchingProcess` with a `Population`. The people the doses protect leave the susceptibles. A `PerGroup` budget doses one type. The new `dosing` field, an `AbstractDosing` defaulting to `RandomDosing()`, sets how a batch of doses is spread. A new rule defines `EpiBranch.protected_by_doses`.
