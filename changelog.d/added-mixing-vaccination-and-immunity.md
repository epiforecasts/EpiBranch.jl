`MixingMeanFieldProcess` and `MixingLinearNoiseProcess` now read a vaccination campaign from the `ModelSpec`'s interventions instead of refusing it: a `GroupVaccination`, alone or under `Scheduled` (a `start_time` and/or `end_time`) and `CapacityConstrained` (a fixed budget or a schedule such as `Steps`), becomes doses into each mixing group's compartments. `group_key` (which must be one of the `mixing_by` keys), `efficacy` and `mode`, `coverage`, `dose_delay` and the budget are read from the intervention, so nothing is given twice. Doses reach people whatever their status, so only the share of a group's doses that falls on unvaccinated susceptibles protects anyone. An all-or-nothing dose makes a share `efficacy` of them immune; a leaky dose moves them to a vaccinated-susceptible compartment whose hazard is scaled by `1 - efficacy`. A mode with no such model, a `waning` or `delay_to_immunity`, a `DeferRefused` budget, a `Scheduled` gated on cases, and any other intervention are refused rather than approximated.

How a group's discrete trigger and a shared budget become doses is a choice the caller makes with a new `DoseAllocation`. The default, `TriggeredInPriority`, follows the exact model: a group is triggered once one eligible case is expected, is offered doses `dose_delay` later, and triggered groups draw on the budget in the order they were triggered. `SplitByGroupSize` and `SplitBySusceptibles` instead share the budget's daily rate over the groups, ignoring the trigger. `HybridProcess` hands the trigger times, the doses given and who they protected from its exact phase across the switch to the default.

Both approximations take `immune = ImmuneShare(...)` or `ImmuneCount(...)` in `simulate`, a per-group share or number of people immune at `t = 0`, taken out of the susceptibles before the initial infections. The states gain `V` (dosed susceptibles), `P` (immune) and `D` (doses given) and the progress of the campaign, and the linear noise covariance covers `(S, I, V, P)`, with `S` and `I` first as before.
