`produced_states(transition)` returns the states a progression step produces, `()` by default and `(state,)` for a `Transition`. The engine reads it to open infectiousness windows, validate their `from` and detect `:susceptible_again`, so a custom step declares what it produces by defining a method rather than needing a `state` field. Reinfection is no longer detected by probing custom steps: a step that sets `susceptible_again_time` must declare `:susceptible_again`.
