The `ChainLengths` likelihood and `chain_length_distribution` now refuse a model whose onset-timed `Reporting` step leaves some cases unreported because `prob_asymptomatic > 0`. Previously the full-reporting check used only the probability set on the step, so a step at probability 1 passed. An onset-timed step also needs exactly one `clinical_presentation` with a fixed `prob_asymptomatic`. Otherwise the model is refused with an `ArgumentError`.
