`weekly_incidence(states)` gives the weekly case counts of a vector of simulated outbreaks as one table with a `sim_id` column, counting a week with no cases as 0. `summarise(df; value, by, probs)` then returns quantiles of a column across outbreaks for each value of `by`, such as the median with 50% and 95% bands of weekly incidence, or the quantiles of outbreak size from `chain_statistics(states)`.
