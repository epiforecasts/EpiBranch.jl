`PerGroup` gives a `CapacityConstrained` a budget of its own for each group, as a number or a schedule: `budget_per_period = PerGroup(:under5 => 10.0, :over5 => Steps(...))`. It is part of the intervention rather than of one engine, so the individual-based simulation, `simulate_by_generation`, `MixingMeanFieldProcess`, `MixingLinearNoiseProcess` and `HybridProcess` all read it. The groups are the values of a `GroupVaccination`'s `group_key`, or of the `key` the `PerGroup` names for an intervention without one, and a group missing from the mapping is an `ArgumentError`. An extension author can give an intervention a group of its own with `EpiBranch.budget_group_key`. A new page, "What each engine supports", tabulates which engine applies which intervention, and `test/test_engine_support.jl` checks each engine against it.
