`epicurve` plots the weekly cases of simulated outbreaks, a median with 50% and 95% bands over many runs or bars for one, and `chain_size_plot` plots simulated chain sizes against their exact law. Both return a Makie `Figure` and live in the new `EpiBranchMakieExt` extension, loaded with `using Makie` or a backend such as `using CairoMakie`. Makie is a weak dependency, which adds nothing to the core dependencies.
