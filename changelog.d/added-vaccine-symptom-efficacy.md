Vaccine doses take a `symptom_efficacy`: the probability that a vaccinated person who is infected, whose onset would fall after becoming immune, and who would have developed symptoms, stays asymptomatic instead. `is_asymptomatic` and the onset time reflect it, so onset-triggered isolation and contact tracing see fewer symptomatic cases among the vaccinated. `symptom_efficacy(ind)` reads the value drawn for a person.
