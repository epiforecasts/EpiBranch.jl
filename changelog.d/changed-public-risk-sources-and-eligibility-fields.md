`EpiBranch.HostImmunity` and `EpiBranch.AbortedInfection` are now `public`, and `EpiBranch.infinite_draw_is_final` is a documented kernel trait: a kernel written outside the package that returns `Inf` once its total hazard is exhausted declares it to skip the bisection retry. `Isolation` now takes its required fields from `required_fields(eligibility)` (with `required_fields(::SymptomaticOnly) = [:asymptomatic]`), so a custom `IsolationEligibility` extends the public `required_fields`; the internal `_required_for_eligibility` is gone.
