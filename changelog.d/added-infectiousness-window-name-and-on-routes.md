`Infectiousness` takes a `name`, so a `BranchingProcess` built from several
windows — community and household contact, say — reaches `risk_applies` as
distinctly named routes instead of all showing up as `:transmission`. Wrapping
an intervention in the new `OnRoutes` then scopes it to the named routes it
should act on: isolation can take a case out of community contact while it
goes on infecting the people it lives with.
