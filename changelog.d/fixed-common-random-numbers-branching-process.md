`CommonRandomNumbers` now keeps a branching process's two scenarios coupled when an intervention prevents an infection. A case is identified by its infector and its place among that infector's contacts rather than by infection order, and its offspring count and generation times come from streams keyed the same way, so a prevented infection removes only its own subtree. `make_contact!` takes an optional `place` keyword, and `simulate(model, n)` gives each replicate its own `CommonRandomNumbers` streams.
