The negative binomial chain-size log-likelihood and `extinction_probability(R, k)` no longer lose precision once `k` is about 1e10 or larger. The gamma ratio in the `GammaBorel` and `PoissonGammaChainSize` log-densities is now a finite sum of logs, combined term by term with the power terms. The negative binomial probability generating function uses `log1p`. Neither subtracts large, nearly equal numbers any more. The log-likelihood stays at the Poisson limit instead of drifting away from it and turning positive. `extinction_probability(R, k)` no longer builds a `NegativeBinomial`, because its stored `p` rounds `R` away. A `NegativeBinomial` passed in still stores only `p`, and its mean is only as accurate as that.
