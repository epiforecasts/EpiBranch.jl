**Breaking:** `containment_probability` now returns `(; n_contained, n, proportion, lower, upper)`, with a Wilson score interval at `level` (default 0.95) for simulation error, in place of a bare proportion. Use `.proportion` for the previous value. `scenario_sweep` still reports the proportion in its `containment_probability` column. The new `containment_difference(a, b; paired, level)` gives the difference in containment between two sets of runs with an interval, for paired proportions (`paired = true`, runs sharing random numbers) or independent ones.
