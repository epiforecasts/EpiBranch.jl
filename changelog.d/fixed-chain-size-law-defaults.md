A user-written `ChainSizeLaw` with only a `logpdf` method now gets `pdf`, `minimum`, `maximum`, `insupport`, `cdf` and `logccdf` from the parent type. `cdf` no longer overflows the stack and `minimum` no longer throws a `MethodError`. The built-in laws drop their identical copies and keep only the methods that differ, such as the support of `TruncatedChainSize` and the `cdf` and `logccdf` of `HittingTimeChainSize`.
