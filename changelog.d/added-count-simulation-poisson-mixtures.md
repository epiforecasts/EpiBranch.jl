`simulate` also runs a branching process with a `PoissonLognormal` or `PoissonWeibull` offspring law as daily counts. The total infectiousness of a cohort is the sum of one draw per member. The state keeps these draws to share out the infections of the cohort when its people are built.
The `CountRule` constructor takes the distribution `ν` of one member's infectiousness in place of a gamma shape `k`; `CountRule(R, k, g)` with a number `k` still works. For these two laws the count-level path costs time and memory in proportion to the number of cases, as the draws of the members are kept.
