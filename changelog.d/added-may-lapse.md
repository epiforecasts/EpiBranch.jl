`EpiBranch.may_lapse(intervention)` is a public trait saying whether a block an intervention composes could later be lifted, which a continuous-time race consults before it stops proposing contacts for a pair. `InterventionWrapper` is now public and forwards it, so a wrapper written outside the package, such as a seasonal closure, can declare `may_lapse` true instead of inheriting `standing_block` from what it wraps.
