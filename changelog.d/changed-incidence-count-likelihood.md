`loglikelihood(::IncidenceCounts, ...)` is now a renewal count likelihood: given the counts before it, each step's count is negative binomial (Poisson for a Poisson offspring law) with mean `R * Σ_s w[t - s] * C_s` over the cases `C_s` infected at step `s`, and dispersion from the variance of the offspring law, instead of a normal or lognormal matched to the moments. Counts of zero are scored like any other. Reporting probability and delay, multi-type processes and seeds are supported as before. `NormalApproximation`, `LognormalApproximation` and `IncidenceApproximation` are removed, and `loglikelihood` no longer takes an approximation argument. `incidence_moments` is unchanged. The "From a case time series" section of the inference tutorial is rewritten for the new likelihood.
