`logccdf` of the chain-size laws, the `prob_concluded` mixture likelihood and open-ended `ChainSizeClasses` no longer return `-Inf` for a large chain under a subcritical model whose tail probability is below about `1e-16`. Once the cumulative sum passes one half, the tail is summed upwards in log space, with the infinite-chain mass added for a supercritical `Borel` or `GammaBorel`.
