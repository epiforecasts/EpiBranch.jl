`initialise_state(model, cases)` builds a `SimulationState` from a table of observed cases (infection times, and optionally infectors, onsets and isolation times) on a single-window `BranchingProcess` or a `ModelSpec` of one. `simulate(model, state; max_time)` then projects the outbreak forward from it, drawing each observed case's later contacts from the offspring law conditioned on the contacts already listed.
