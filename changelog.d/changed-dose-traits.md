`EpiBranch.count_dosing` and `EpiBranch.GroupDosing` are replaced by one small trait per fact: `EpiBranch.gives_group_doses` opts an intervention in, and `EpiBranch.dose_group_key`, `dose_efficacy`, `dose_mode`, `dose_coverage`, `dose_delay`, `dose_delay_to_immunity` and `dose_waning` give the facts of its dose, with defaults for all but the first two. `GroupVaccination` defines them. An intervention that does not opt in is still refused by `simulate_by_generation` and the mixing approximations, and `simulate` on a model with it runs as an individual simulation.
