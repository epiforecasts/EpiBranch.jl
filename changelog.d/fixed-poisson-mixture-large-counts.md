`pdf`, `cdf` and `ccdf` of `PoissonLognormal` and `PoissonWeibull` now integrate over the log of the rate, split at the mode of the integrand. They were wrong for counts of about 100 and above, where the integral missed the narrow peak. Both laws are in the benchmark suite, and the tests compare them with reference values from superspreading and, at large counts, with direct integration.
