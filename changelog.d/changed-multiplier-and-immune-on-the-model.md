`multiplier` is now a keyword of `BranchingProcess` and `immune` a keyword of `Population`, in place of keywords of `simulate_by_generation` and of `simulate(model, state::GenerationCounts)`, which no longer take them. An individual run of a model with a `multiplier` draws it once per generation and scales the offspring mean of every case in that generation, with the same law for the counts of a generation as `simulate_by_generation`. The people in a population's `immune` are never infected in an individual run, and a model with either no longer takes the daily count-level path. A model other than a `BranchingProcess` raises an error for a nonzero `immune`.
