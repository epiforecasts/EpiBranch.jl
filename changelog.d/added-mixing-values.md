`FrequencyDependent(contacts)` and `DensityDependent(rates)` give how groups mix without their sizes, and `BranchingProcess(mixing, q, D, dist_fn, gt; population)` and `MixingProcess(; mixing, q, population, mixing_by)` take one in the same way. Each derives what it needs from the mixing value and the population: the offspring means `c[j,i] * q * D` or `r[j,i] * N_i * q * D` for a branching process, and the rate per pair `c[j,i] * q / N_i` or `r[j,i] * q` for a finite population, so the group sizes enter only where the model uses them. A density-dependent branching process needs the sizes even without depletion. `mixing_rate(contacts, n)` and a `rate(from, to)` callable remain for an arbitrary per-pair rate.

`MixingMeanFieldProcess` and its linear-noise counterpart read the group sizes straight from a population with sizes by group, and `MeanFieldProcess` and `LinearNoiseProcess` take `population`.
