`EpiBranch.time_aware`, `resolves_under_capacity`, `discovers_actions`, `proposes_actions` and `has_competing_risk` are public traits, each defaulting to what the engine used to infer from the methods a type defines, so an intervention written outside the package can declare the answer instead of adding or removing a method. They decide whether `Scheduled` runs and undoes an intervention or gates it up front, whether `CapacityConstrained` and `HeldCapacity` ration a direct effect or the action funnel, whether an intervention is offered candidates, and whether the race keeps fallback proposals.
