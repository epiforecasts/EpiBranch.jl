`ConditionOn` and `RecruitedIndex` now live in EpiBranch, with a new `ExplainIndex` rule that conditions on nobody, and `compile_contact_pairs` takes `condition_on` in place of `explain_index`. `explain_index = false` is `condition_on = RecruitedIndex()`, and `explain_index = true` is `condition_on = ExplainIndex()`. The default is still `ExplainIndex()` with `external = true` and `RecruitedIndex()` without. A rule of your own defines `EpiBranch.condition_mask`, which replaces `EpiHouseholds.condition_mask`. `compile_household_pairs` takes the same `condition_on` and `EpiHouseholds` keeps `EarliestInfected`.
