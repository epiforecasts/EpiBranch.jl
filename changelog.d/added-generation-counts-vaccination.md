`simulate_by_generation` and its continuation now take a `ModelSpec` built on a `BranchingProcess` and apply its `GroupVaccination` campaigns, under `CapacityConstrained` (including a schedule budget) and `Scheduled`, to the susceptibles of each type between generations. The doses of generation `g` cover `[g * generation_length, (g + 1) * generation_length)`, each type has the whole budget to itself, a dose reaches the susceptibles of a type in proportion to their share of its population, and protects with probability `efficacy`. A model with an intervention this cannot apply, or with no `generation_length`, raises an error naming it.
