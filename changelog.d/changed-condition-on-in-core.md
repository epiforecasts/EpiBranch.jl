`ConditionOn`, `RecruitedIndex` and `EarliestInfected` now live in EpiBranch and work on any contact structure, with `condition_mask` defined for an `InfectionLayer`. `compile_contact_pairs` and `compile_household_pairs` take `external_hazard` (the hazard the layout will be evaluated with) in place of `external` and `explain_index`, and the single-argument `compile_contact_pairs(data)` takes `condition_on`. `EarliestInfected` picks the earliest infected person in each connected part of the contact structure, each household for households.
