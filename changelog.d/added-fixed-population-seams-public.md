A structure written outside the package can now take `initial_cases` and get a checked infection likelihood through public seams. `EpiBranch.FixedPopulationProcess` is a supertype with the defaults the built-in fixed-population processes shared: `honours_termination_controls` is `false`, `validate_process_windows` checks the window's `until` states, and `process_window` reads the window from the fields `from` and `until` or from a method of its own, which `infection_layer_columns` uses too. `EpiBranch.supports_initial_cases` is a trait, `false` unless a model says otherwise, that `validate_initial_cases` consults; `validate_initial_case_ids` and `seed_initial_cases!` check and apply the IDs. `infection_layer_columns`, `layer_host_times`, `validate_infection_likelihood`, `validate_removal_gap_keys` and `warn_uncovered_terminal_states` are public under the names the underscored internals had, as are `REMOVAL_STRETCHES_KEY` and `LEAKY_REMOVAL_STRETCHES_KEY`, the keys under which the removal-stretch columns are read. `HomogeneousProcess`, `MixingProcess`, `NetworkProcess` and `HouseholdProcess` subtype `FixedPopulationProcess`.
