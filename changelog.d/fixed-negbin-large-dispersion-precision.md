The negative binomial chain-size log-likelihood and `extinction_probability(R, k)` no longer lose precision once `k` is about 1e10 or larger. The gamma ratio in the `GammaBorel` and `PoissonGammaChainSize` log-densities is now a finite sum of logs combined term by term with the power terms, and the negative binomial probability generating function uses `log1p`, so nothing subtracts large nearly equal numbers. The log-likelihood now stays at the Poisson limit instead of drifting away from it and turning positive, and `extinction_probability(R, k)` no longer builds a `NegativeBinomial`, whose stored `p` rounds `R` away. A `NegativeBinomial` passed in still carries only `p`, so its mean is only as accurate as that.
