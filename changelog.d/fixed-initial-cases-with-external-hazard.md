Chosen `initial_cases` on a household or network model with an `external_hazard` are now infected at day 0 whatever the risks. A vaccinated seeded case is no longer blocked and redrawn from the hazard. Seeded cases are recorded with `:seeded` set rather than as community introductions. `HouseholdInfections`, `NetworkInfections` and `RoutedNetworkInfections` have a `seeded` mask that `compile_contact_pairs` conditions on (see `seeded_hosts`). The likelihood therefore takes seeded cases as given, as it does without a hazard, instead of adding a community introduction for each and inflating the hazard estimate.
