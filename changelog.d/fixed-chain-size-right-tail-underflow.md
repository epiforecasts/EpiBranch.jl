`logccdf` of the chain-size laws, the `prob_concluded` mixture likelihood and open-ended `ChainSizeClasses` no longer return `-Inf` for a large chain under a subcritical model whose tail probability is below about `1e-16`. Once the tail falls below about `1e-4`, these now sum it upwards in log space. For a supercritical `Borel` or `GammaBorel` they also add the infinite-chain mass.
