`EpiBranch.INTERVENTION_REMOVAL` is gone from `until`, which now holds only states of the natural history. A route that the interventions in the model end is built with `RouteWindow(...; removed_by_interventions = true)` instead of listing the pseudo-state. `EpiBranch.removed_by_interventions(route)` reads it back (`false` for `nothing`). An intervention that scoped itself to such routes through `INTERVENTION_REMOVAL in route.until` should call `removed_by_interventions(route)`.
