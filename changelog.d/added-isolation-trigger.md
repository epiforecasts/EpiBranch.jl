`Isolation` takes a `trigger`, a dispatched seam for what the isolation delay is counted from. `AfterSymptomOnset()` is the default and keeps the current behaviour. `AfterPositiveTest()` isolates a case at its recorded `:test_time` plus the delay, which then stands for the turnaround, including cases without symptoms when paired with `eligibility = AllCases()`. A trigger of your own is a subtype of `IsolationTrigger` with a method of `isolation_reference_time`.
