Reverse-mode gradients now work through the model constructors and likelihood wrappers (`loglikelihood` with a model, `chain_size_distribution`, `chain_length_distribution`, `offspring_distribution`) under Mooncake and Enzyme, via new package extensions. A bare model's `progression` is now the empty tuple. A likelihood estimated by simulation raises an error under Enzyme in reverse mode. Enzyme does not yet differentiate the closed-form likelihood of a `ModelSpec`.
