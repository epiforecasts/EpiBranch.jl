A continuous-time race now checks a `PairKernel`'s `watches` against what its
projection actually reads, rather than trusting the declaration: after a case's
interventions resolve, it re-projects every host a pending or future draw
still reads and throws, naming the route, when a record has changed although
none of the declared keys did. A projection reading more than it declares
used to keep drawing from the stale rate it was first drawn at, with no
warning and no error.
