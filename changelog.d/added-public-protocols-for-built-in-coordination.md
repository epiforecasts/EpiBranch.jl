Added public hooks for the channels through which built-in interventions coordinate, so an intervention written outside the package can take part. `EpiBranch.admit_trace_actions!(tracer, state, actions)` admits a tracer's follow-up actions through whatever `CapacityConstrained` or `HeldCapacity` wraps it. `EpiBranch.is_ring_hop(infector)` tells a tracer it is called for a member a ring is passing through. `EpiBranch.flag_for_isolation!(individual, time)` and `EpiBranch.traced_isolation_time(individual)` write and read the "traced, isolate at onset" hand-over that `FlagOnly`, `Isolation` and `RingVaccination` share. `EpiBranch.reported_value(::Val{key}, individual)` sets what `linelist` shows for a key, next to `event_time_metadata`; the isolation columns now use it, and `event_time_metadata` reports `requires_infection = false` for the two isolation times, as the line list already treated them.
