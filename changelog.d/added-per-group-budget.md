`PerGroup(:under5 => 10.0, :over5 => Steps(...))` gives each group of the model's `Population` its own budget, as a number or a schedule. `CapacityConstrained` takes it as `budget_per_period` and `HeldCapacity` as `capacity`, so any intervention with a budget, including one written outside the package, has it with no code of its own. A group of the population missing from the mapping, or a name that is no group of it, is an error. Every model reads it the same way: the individual-based simulation, `simulate_by_generation`, the mixing approximations and `HybridProcess`. `capacity_usage` and `held_usage` take a group's name as `key`.
