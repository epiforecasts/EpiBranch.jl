Interventions with budgets and schedules ​

An intervention with a dose budget or a schedule behaves the same in every model that applies it. This page describes a model by four components: the sharing of its budget, the timing of its schedule, the treatment of a person the budget turns away, and the groups it reads. simulate on a model of individuals, such as BranchingProcess, HomogeneousProcess, MixingProcess or a network or household model, applies every intervention. simulate_by_generation, MixingMeanFieldProcess, MixingLinearNoiseProcess and HybridProcess work on groups rather than people. They apply only the interventions listed under "What a model of groups applies". Any other intervention raises an ArgumentError naming it and pointing to simulate on a model of individuals.

One budget, or one per group ​

A dose budget limits the reach of an intervention. Given as a number or a schedule, budget_per_period is one pool shared by every group, in every model. A PerGroup gives each group of the Population of the model its own budget, as a number or a schedule:

julia
doses = PerGroup(
    :under5 => 10.0,
    :over5 => Steps([7.0, 14.0], [0.0, 50.0, 0.0]),
)
campaign = CapacityConstrained(
    GroupVaccination(efficacy = 0.9, group_key = :age);
    budget_per_period = doses, period = 1.0
)
process = MixingProcess(
    population = Population([300, 700]; groups = ["under5", "over5"]),
    mixing_by = (:age,), mixing = FrequencyDependent([0.5 0.1; 0.1 0.5])
)

The groups are those of the population: the groups of a grouped population, or 1, 2, ... when it names none. A group of the population missing from the mapping is an error. An unknown name is also an error, so a mistyped name cannot pass as a scenario with no doses. To give a group no doses, give it a budget of 0. A population with no groups has none to name. PerGroup raises an error there. PerGroup also sets the places of a HeldCapacity per group. It works with every intervention with a budget, including one written outside the package.

Schedule and refusal policy ​

A Scheduled with a start_time and end_time limits an intervention to a calendar window, in every model. A Scheduled that starts after a number of cases, or that waits on a trigger or a predicate, depends on individuals. Only simulate on a model of individuals applies it. By default CapacityConstrained drops a person the budget turns away (on_refusal = DropRefused()), in every model. on_refusal = DeferRefused() books that person into a later period. Only simulate on a model of individuals does this.

Support in models of groups ​

simulate_by_generation, the mean-field and linear-noise approximations and HybridProcess apply a GroupVaccination, or in simulate_by_generation a MassVaccination with a dose budget, alone or under Scheduled (a start_time and end_time only) and CapacityConstrained (one per campaign, with a shared or PerGroup budget and on_refusal = DropRefused()). They also name any input they cannot represent, such as a distribution as efficacy, a delay_to_immunity or waning. The docstring of each model lists them. RingVaccination, a MassVaccination in the approximations, Isolation, ContactTracing, HeldCapacity and any other intervention need simulate on a model of individuals.

An intervention written outside the package is applied to groups by defining EpiBranch.gives_group_doses as true and the EpiBranch.dose_* facts of its dose; see its docstring. Without it, it needs simulate on a model of individuals.

The test file test/test_engine_support.jl runs every kind of model against this page. If a model reads a different input, the test fails until the page is updated.
