The closed forms now recognise a reporting step and an onset builder by trait instead of by type. Defining `EpiBranch.reporting_probability` and `EpiBranch.reporting_from` makes a progression transition of your own a reporting step for the chain-size law, `end_of_outbreak_probability` and the incidence likelihood. Defining `EpiBranch.onset_law` lets an attribute of your own supply the incubation period and asymptomatic share that the analytic effect of `Isolation` and reporting timed from onset read. `Reporting` and `clinical_presentation` define them, so existing models behave as before.
